# Evidence for Reviewer RsA418

## Validation boundary

DFJSP is simplified from an anonymized automotive machining/assembly workflow; RCPSP is simplified from the installation schedule of a major industrial apparatus. Routing, precedence, resource, duration, and due-date relations are retained. Disturbances are script-generated so all methods receive the same seed and causal event stream. The evaluation validates the MES/CPS scheduling layer, not raw-IIoT recognition or a live closed-loop deployment.
